Full Logo

SIM SIM
Story moment: Ali Baba (or the seeker) reaches toward the ornate door. The gesture says "I'm about to call the magic words." Golden sparkles suggest the moment before the unlock. The door opens behind him with light pouring through.

Design Elements

Character: Simplified figure in traditional Middle Eastern dress (robes, turban). Reaching hand toward door — active, intentional gesture.

Door: Ornate arched frame with wooden panels. Shows craftsmanship, tradition, history.

Opening: Right door swings open with light — represents the magic moment when words are spoken.

Magic/Sparkles: Golden dots around character suggest the supernatural moment about to happen.

Colors: Warm earth tones (brown, tan) for authenticity. Gold for magic. Matches all theme backgrounds.

Adapts to All Themes

The character and door work across all color themes — the human element and narrative stay constant while colors shift to match each theme's palette.
