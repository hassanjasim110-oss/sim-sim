        How listening works

        Listening works while Sim Sim is open in Chrome.

        Sim Sim hears you through Chrome's built-in voice typing. To turn your voice into words, Chrome sends it to Google's speech service — that's how the free built-in tool works, and it happens whether or not you say the name. Words without your assistant's name never reach Sim Sim's server or its brain, and Sim Sim never saves them — but they do pass through Google first. Want the mic off except when you choose? Turn Listening off and type instead — or use push-to-talk, where the mic only runs while you hold the button (it uses the same Google service while it runs).

              Terms

              Sim Sim is an early prototype, built by Hassan Ali for the Meta AI developer hackathon. It can be wrong.

              For anything that matters — law, money, health, deadlines — check the official source. Sim Sim shows its source for precise answers, and says so when it has none.

              Sim Sim is not for emergencies, and it is not a lawyer, doctor or financial adviser. Use it for your own personal, lawful use.

              Privacy

              What is stored, and where. Your profile, your notebook (what Sim Sim learned about you), open loops, settings, chat history, morning briefing, Discussion picks, pictures you asked Sim Sim to draw, the face you chose for Sim Sim (a picture you upload as its face is saved so it can be shown), and saved logins. All of it is stored on this Sim Sim server, kept separately for each account. Pictures you show Sim Sim in chat and screen snapshots are not saved.

              The brain. Sim Sim's thinking is done by Meta's Model API. To answer, draw pictures and find official web pages, Sim Sim sends your message, and the parts of your notebook it needs, to Meta.

              Listening. Chrome's built-in speech recognition handles the mic first: Chrome sends what the mic hears to Google's speech service to turn it into words. Words without your assistant's name never reach Sim Sim's server or its brain.

              Sign-in uses Google Firebase, which sees your email address.

              Saved logins are encrypted on the server. They are never sent to the brain, never go into your notebook, and are not in your data download.

              Nothing is sold. There are no ads.

              Your control. Download all your data, or erase it with Forget everything, under Data controls. Saved logins are deleted one by one under Saved logins.

              Your picks teach your team your taste. They're saved privately — just yours, never shared.
