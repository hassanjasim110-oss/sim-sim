- **Wake word -> question bar -> auto-send (founder, 2026-10-29): ONE voice-capture flow for the wake word AND the mic button** (public/app.js `captureStart / captureWords / captureEnd`, exposed as `SimSimChat.capture`).
  - **Why it failed:** after "Sim Sim" alone, Sim Sim waited only 0.7 s, then PAUSED the ear to say "I'm here". Restarting the ear took about 2-3 s, so a request said after a normal pause was never heard, and 8 s later the wake quietly ended. Separately, the mic button had its own one-phrase recognizer: it typed into the box but stopped at the first pause and never sent.
  - **The flow:**
    - Whoever hears (the mic button's recognizer, or the doorman's recognizer after the name) hands ALL the words so far to `captureWords`. They are typed live into the question bar, after any text already typed there.
    - The words are rebuilt from the recognizer's results each time, so nothing is ever doubled.
    - Every new piece of speech restarts a 3-second clock (`CAPTURE_QUIET_MS`). When it runs out, the bar is sent ONCE through the Send button's own path (`form.requestSubmit()`), with `viaWake` kept for the briefing and Screen Watch.

  - **Cancelled, never sent (the words stay in the box):** a real typed/pasted edit in the box (`input` event, isTrusted only), pressing the mic button (= Stop, for either source), a sidebar view, New chat, opening a saved chat, Profile/Settings, sign-out.

    - The doorman's own recognizer stays on (no handoff gap). Words after the name in the same breath are kept, and the name never is.
    - If Chrome re-writes the name in a later result, the words already heard after it are kept.
    - If Chrome restarts the recognizer mid-request, the words so far are carried.
